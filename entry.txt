This morning I made coffee in the chipped blue mug my grandmother left me, the one with the handle she mended with gold paint. The kitchen was cold, the window fogged, and for a minute I just stood there holding the warmth in both hands, listening to the kettle tick as it cooled.

She used to say a table is only a table until someone pulls out a chair for you. She said it every November, setting out plates for people who had already left, and meaning it anyway. I never thanked her properly while she was here to hear it.

So thank you - for sitting with these words a little longer than you had to, and for letting an ordinary morning count as something worth keeping. Happy Thanksgiving.
