Thank you for reading this - really reading it, the way you read all of them. Thousands of us wrote you small, hopeful attempts at gratitude, and you give every single one your full attention before you choose. That patience is the first thing worth thanking you for.

Yours is a strange job: you were asked to feel thankful, and then to rank the rest of us by how well we made you feel it. Nobody ever thanks the judge. Winners thank their luck; everyone else questions the draw. So let this entry be the simple thing it is - thanks for your fairness when two texts are nearly equal, for taking seriously words that were written only for you, and for doing carefully a job no one envies.

Whatever you decide, this was worth writing. Happy Thanksgiving, from one of the thousands - grateful that you were listening.
